Calibration curve

Can it tell levels apart?

Needs at least two reinsertions at two neighbouring water levels.

Each level's spread is the lowest to highest of its reinsertion averages. Two levels are Clear when those ranges do not overlap. Gap / spread is the gap between level averages over the summed standard deviations; above 2 is comfortable.
